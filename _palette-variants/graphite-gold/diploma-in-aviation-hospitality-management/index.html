Got Questions?

FAQs on the Hospitality Management Course in Kerala.

What is the qualification for Diploma in Aviation and Hospitality?
You need to be a +2 Pass (Any stream) or a Graduate. The most important requirement is a passion for service and a pleasing personality.
Is this course good for becoming an Air Hostess?
Yes! Airlines love candidates with Hospitality Management skills. This course includes Cabin Crew Training basics and grooming, which gives you a significant advantage in airline interviews.
What is the difference between Aviation Diploma and Hospitality Diploma?
An Aviation Diploma focuses on airport operations (logistics, ramp, security). A Hospitality Diploma focuses on guest service (lounges, hotels, customer care). Our course combines both, giving you double the job opportunities.
Which is the best Aviation college in Kerala for Hospitality?
SkyWings Aviation is rated as the best because of our specific focus on Premium Hospitality. We don't just teach you to serve, we teach you to manage luxury experiences.
What kind of jobs can I get after 6 months?
You can work as a Lounge Hostess, Meet & Greet Officer, Guest Relations Executive in 5-star hotels, or Ground Staff in airlines, all within 6 months of joining.
Does SkyWings offer placement in Airport Lounges?
Yes, we specialise in placing students in premium Airport Lounges. These jobs offer excellent salaries, tips, and a comfortable air-conditioned work environment.
Is the course recognised?
Yes, our curriculum aligns with National Skill Development standards and Aviation of IATA guidelines, ensuring your certificate is valued by employers globally.
Why do you teach AI in a hospitality course?
Because the future is digital. Knowing how to use AI tools for customer service and check-in systems makes you a Modern Professional, distinct from candidates at other institutes.
Can I work abroad with this Diploma?
Absolutely. The Middle East (Dubai, Qatar, Saudi Arabia) has a massive demand for Indian hospitality professionals. Our diploma is widely accepted across these regions.
What is the salary for freshers in Aviation Hospitality?
Freshers can expect between ₹20,000 to ₹35,000 in India. In International Airport Lounges or Airlines abroad, salaries can range from ₹80,000 to ₹1.5 Lakhs per month.
