What is BBA Aviation

BBA Aviation in Kerala, what it means, what it covers

BBA Aviation, officially called BBA in Airline and Airport Management, is a 3-year undergraduate degree programme that prepares students for leadership and management roles in the aviation industry. It is one of the most in-demand professional degrees in Kerala today.

Unlike a general BBA, this programme includes specialised subjects such as Airport Operations and Safety, Aviation Law, Aerodrome Management, DGR (Dangerous Goods Regulations), Passenger Handling Systems, Airline Finance, and IATA-based operational training. Students also receive training in Communicative English, Foreign Languages, and professional grooming, making them airline-interview-ready from graduation day.

At SkyWings Academy in Kochi, Kerala's No.1 BBA Aviation college, the curriculum also includes a Certificate Course in Artificial Intelligence for Aviation, preparing students for the AI-driven airport of 2030.

SkyWings Academy's BBA Aviation syllabus is structured across 3 years to build knowledge progressively, from fundamentals to advanced operations management.

Frequently Asked

Questions about BBA Aviation colleges in Kerala

What is the eligibility for BBA Aviation in Kerala?
You need to have passed Plus Two (Class 12) in any stream, Science, Commerce, or Humanities, with a minimum pass percentage. No entrance exam like NEET or JEE is required. Admission at SkyWings is merit-based with a personal interview.
What is the salary after BBA Aviation in Kerala?
BBA Aviation freshers in India typically earn ₹20,000 to ₹45,000 per month in roles like Ground Staff Executive, Airport Duty Officer, or Airline Operations Executive. Gulf-based positions offer ₹80,000 to ₹2 Lakhs per month, often tax-free.
Is BBA Aviation better than regular BBA in Kerala?
BBA Aviation is more specialised and career-focused for students targeting the aviation industry. It includes all standard BBA subjects plus specialised modules in Airport Operations, IATA Safety Standards, Aviation Law, and Airline Finance.
Can Commerce students join BBA Aviation colleges in Kerala?
Yes. Commerce students are among the strongest BBA Aviation candidates because the programme's Business Administration modules, Airline Finance, Marketing, and Supply Chain, align naturally with a Commerce background.
How many years is BBA Aviation in Kerala?
BBA Aviation is a 3-year full-time undergraduate degree programme. At SkyWings Academy in Kochi and Mahe, Kerala, the degree is affiliated to Pondicherry University and/or Bharathiar University, both UGC-recognised institutions.
What jobs can I get after BBA Aviation in Kerala?
After completing BBA Aviation from SkyWings Academy, graduates can work as Airport Manager, Ground Staff Executive, Airline Operations Officer, Cargo Coordinator, Customer Service Executive, Terminal Duty Manager, or Logistics Manager, in India, the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and other countries.
Is there a BBA Aviation college in Kerala with hostel facilities?
SkyWings Academy does not have an on-campus hostel, but our admissions team actively assists outstation students in finding safe, affordable, and verified PG accommodation near our Kochi campus.
