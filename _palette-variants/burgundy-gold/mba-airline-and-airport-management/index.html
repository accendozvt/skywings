Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions.

Is the MBA degree recognised by the Government?
Yes. The MBA degree is awarded by a UGC Recognised Government University (such as Bharathiar University), ensuring it is valid for government jobs, UPSC exams, and migration abroad.
Why is SkyWings considered the best MBA Aviation college in Kerala?
We combine a Government University degree with practical IATA-based training and a cutting-edge AI in Aviation syllabus. Our focus on leadership development and 100% internship support sets us apart from every other college in Kerala.
What is the salary after MBA in Aviation Management?
Managerial roles in aviation are highly paid. Freshers can expect ₹40,000 to ₹60,000 per month, while experienced Airport Managers earn significantly higher, often in lakhs, especially in the Middle East.
Can I join if I have a B.Tech or B.Com degree?
Yes. Graduates from any discipline, Arts, Science, Commerce, or Engineering, with a minimum of 55% marks are eligible. Engineering graduates often excel in technical aviation management roles.
Does the course cover Logistics?
Yes. The syllabus includes Air Cargo, Shipping, and Warehouse Management, making you eligible for high-paying jobs in the logistics and supply chain sector as well as aviation.
Is there an entrance exam?
Admission is generally based on your graduation marks and a personal interview at SkyWings. Please contact our admissions desk for specific university entrance requirements for the current year.
How does AI training help in an MBA?
By learning AI for Aviation Business, you gain skills in data analytics and automation, making you a more efficient and modern manager compared to graduates from traditional colleges who have no AI exposure.
Do you provide placement for MBA students?
Yes, we offer Placement Assistance, connecting MBA graduates with HR departments of leading airlines, airports, and logistics companies for managerial and executive trainee roles.
What is the duration of the internship?
The course includes a mandatory 3-Month Practical Internship. This is crucial for gaining the On-the-Job experience that employers specifically look for when hiring for managerial roles.
Can I work abroad after this course?
Absolutely. The MBA in Airline and Airport Management is a globally recognised degree. The Middle East, Europe, and Canada have a high demand for skilled aviation managers, and your UGC-recognised degree is valid internationally.
