Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions.

What is the scope of BBA Logistics in Kerala?
Kerala is a major logistics hub with ports like Vizhinjam and airports like CIAL. The demand for skilled logistics professionals in the state is at an all-time high, especially with the rapid growth of e-commerce.
Why is SkyWings the best college for BBA Logistics?
We are the only college offering a Certificate in AI for Logistics alongside the standard BBA. Our focus on a 3-month practical internship and Air Cargo training makes our students more employable than others.
Can Arts / Science students join this course?
Yes! BBA Logistics is open to students from any stream, +2 Science, Commerce, or Humanities. You just need a logical mind and a willingness to learn how the world moves.
What is the salary after BBA Logistics?
Freshers typically start between ₹25,000 to ₹40,000 per month. With 5+ years of experience, Supply Chain Managers can earn ₹12 Lakhs to ₹18 Lakhs per annum.
Does this course cover Shipping and Ports?
Yes. Our syllabus includes Commercial Geography, Shipping, and Ocean Freight procedures, making you eligible for jobs in seaports, shipping lines, and freight forwarding companies.
Is the degree recognised for government jobs?
Absolutely. You receive a UGC Recognised Government University Certificate, which is valid for UPSC, SSC, banking exams, and higher studies abroad (MBA / MSc).
How does AI training help in Logistics?
Companies today use AI for everything from route planning to stock prediction. By learning these tools at SkyWings, you become Future-Ready and highly attractive to top recruiters like Amazon and FedEx.
Do you provide internship support?
Yes, we provide 100% assistance for the 3-month practical internship. We place students in reputed logistics companies, shipping firms, or cargo agencies to gain real-world experience.
Can I work abroad with this degree?
Yes. Logistics is a global profession. The principles of Supply Chain (Incoterms, Cargo handling) are the same worldwide. The Middle East and European countries have a huge demand for Indian logistics graduates.
Is there an entrance exam for admission?
Admission is generally based on merit (+2 marks) and a Personal Interview. Early application is strongly recommended as seats in Kerala's No. 1 Logistics College fill up fast.
