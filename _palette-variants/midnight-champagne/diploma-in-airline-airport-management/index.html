Welcome to SkyWings Aviation College, where we turn aspirations into high-flying realities. Got Questions?

FAQs on Airport Management Courses in Kerala.

Is SkyWings the best Aviation college in Kerala for placement?
Yes, SkyWings Aviation is renowned for its genuine placement support. We have tie-ups with leading airlines, ground handling agencies, and hospitality chains. Our alumni are currently working in major airports across India and the UAE.
What is the duration of the Diploma in Airline and Airport Management?
This is a 1-Year intensive programme designed to make you job-ready quickly. It covers both theoretical studies and practical training modules across all core areas of aviation.
Does this course cover IATA standards?
Absolutely. Our curriculum is aligned with Aviation of IATA standards, ensuring that the knowledge you gain is recognised and respected globally, giving you an edge in international job markets.
Can I become an Air Hostess after this Diploma?
Yes. The course includes a dedicated Cabin Crew Training module involving grooming, mock flight scenarios, and safety drills, specifically designed to help you crack Cabin Crew interviews.
Why is AI included in an Aviation Diploma?
The industry is shifting towards automation (DigiYatra, smart check-in). By including a Certificate Course in AI for Aviation, we ensure our students are tech-savvy and future-proof, with a massive advantage over candidates from other institutes.
What is the qualification required to join?
You need to have passed your Plus Two (10+2) in any stream. Graduates and students pursuing graduation can also apply to add a professional certification to their profile.
Do you provide foreign language training?
Yes, we provide basic training in foreign languages like German or French. This is a huge asset for students looking to work with international airlines or in European airports.
Is the uniform included in the fees?
Yes, a professional uniform and grooming guidance are included. We believe wearing a uniform instills the discipline and pride required for an aviation professional from day one.
Can boys apply for Cabin Crew training?
Certainly! The role of Flight Steward is open to men, and many international airlines actively recruit male crew members. Our training is fully open to all eligible candidates.
Where is SkyWings Aviation located?
We are located in Kochi, Kerala. Our strategic location near a major international airport hub provides our students with excellent industry exposure and training opportunities.
