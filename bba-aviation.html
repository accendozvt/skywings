Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions about BBA Aviation in Kerala.

Which is the best college for BBA Aviation in Kerala?
Sky Wings Academy in Kochi is rated as the No.1 Premium Aviation College in Kerala. We offer a unique blend of university syllabus, IATA-based training, and practical airport internships that no other institute in Kerala can match.
What is the difference between BBA Aviation and BBA Airline & Airport Management?
They are often used interchangeably. However, our BBA With Aviation covers both aspects: the business side (Marketing, HR, Finance) and the technical side (Airport Operations, Airside Safety).
Is AI training included in the BBA Aviation course?
Yes! Sky Wings Academy is the only institute offering a Certificate Course in AI for Aviation alongside your degree. You will learn about DigiYatra, smart security, and data analytics in aviation.
Can I get a job in the airport after BBA Aviation?
Absolutely. Graduates can work as Ground Staff, Customer Service Executives, Terminal Managers, and in Cargo Operations. Our 100% Placement Assistance ensures you get interview opportunities at the best companies.
Is BBA Aviation a good career for girls?
It is an excellent career choice. Women have tremendous opportunities in Cabin Crew, Ground Staff, Guest Relations, and HR roles within airlines. The industry is known for being safe, professional, and globally diverse.
What is the salary after BBA Aviation in India?
Freshers can expect salaries between ₹25,000 to ₹45,000 per month. With experience, salaries can go up to ₹1 Lakh+ per month, especially in international airlines and airport management roles.
Do I need to be good at English to join?
English is essential for aviation. But don't worry if you are not fluent yet. Our course includes intensive Communicative English training to ensure you are interview-ready by the time you graduate.
Can Commerce students apply for BBA Aviation?
Yes, students from any stream (+2 Commerce, Science, or Humanities) are eligible. In fact, Commerce students often excel in the management and finance modules of the course.
Does SkyWings Academy provide hostel facilities?
We do not have an on-campus hostel, but we assist outstation students in finding safe, affordable, and high-quality PG accommodation near our Kochi campus.
Why is SkyWings called a "Premium" Aviation College?
