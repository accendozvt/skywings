FAQs — Diploma in Cabin Crew at SkyWings Kerala.

Which is the best college for Cabin Crew Diploma in Kerala?
SkyWings Aviation College is rated as the No.1 Aviation college in Kerala for Cabin Crew training. Our blend of practical simulation, AI training, and placement support makes us the top choice for aspiring flight attendants.
What is the age limit for Cabin Crew jobs?
Most airlines hire freshers between the ages of 18 and 28. Our Diploma in Cabin Crew Operations is designed to get you job-ready within this prime age window.
Is AI training important for Cabin Crew?
Yes. Airlines are increasingly using AI for recruitment and passenger service. Our Certificate Course in AI for Aviation ensures you are digitally prepared, giving you a significant advantage over candidates from other institutes.
Can I join if I have a visible scar or tattoo?
Airlines generally require no visible tattoos or scars while in uniform. During our admission process, we provide a free grooming assessment to guide you honestly on your eligibility.
Does this course cover Hospitality Management?
Yes. Cabin Crew is essentially Hospitality in the Sky. Our syllabus covers extensive Hospitality Diploma modules, making you eligible for jobs in 5-star hotels and luxury resorts as well.
Do I need to know swimming?
For international airlines, swimming is often mandatory. That is why SkyWings includes Swimming Sessions as part of the course — to ensure you are eligible for every major airline.
What is the salary of a Cabin Crew member?
Freshers in domestic airlines earn between ₹45,000 to ₹60,000. In international airlines like Emirates and Qatar Airways, tax-free salaries can range from ₹1.5 Lakh to ₹2.5 Lakhs per month.
Can boys apply for this course?
Absolutely. The role of Flight Steward is open to men, and many international airlines actively recruit male crew members. SkyWings welcomes both male and female candidates equally.
Do you offer placement support?
Yes, we offer 100% Placement Assistance. Our dedicated placement cell works tirelessly to arrange interviews and groom you specifically for each airline's unique requirements.
Why is the course duration 1 year?
One year is the ideal time to transform a student. It allows us to cover the full IATA syllabus, conduct multiple mock interviews, teach a foreign language, and ensure your grooming and presentation become second nature — not an act.
