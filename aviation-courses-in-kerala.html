Common Questions

FAQs on Aviation Courses in Kerala

Everything you need to know about our Aviation Courses

What are the best Aviation Courses to take after 12th (Plus Two)?
After 12th, you have excellent options depending on your career goals. If you want a managerial degree, the BBA with Aviation (3 Years) is the top choice. If you want to fly, the Diploma in Cabin Crew Management (1 Year) is ideal. For a quick entry into ground staff roles, the 6-month or 1-year Diplomas are perfect.
Can Commerce or Arts students enroll in Aviation Courses?
Yes, absolutely! You do not need a science or mathematics background to join our Aviation Courses. Students from any stream (Commerce, Humanities, or Science) with a minimum of 50% marks can apply for our BBA, MBA, and Diploma programs.
Does Skywings provide placement after completing these Aviation Courses?
Yes, Skywings Academy provides 100% Placement Assistance. We conduct mock interviews, HR grooming sessions, and exclusive campus recruitment drives to connect our students with leading airlines, ground handling agencies, and logistics companies.
Why should I choose Aviation Courses that include AI (Artificial Intelligence)?
The aviation industry is rapidly digitising. Technologies like facial recognition at boarding gates and AI-driven baggage tracking are the new normal. By choosing Aviation Courses with an AI-integrated syllabus at Skywings, you graduate with modern tech skills that make you highly preferred by top recruiters.
What is the salary expectation after completing Aviation Courses in Kerala?
Starting salaries vary by role. Freshers in ground staff and logistics typically start between ₹25,000 to ₹40,000 per month. Cabin crew members for domestic airlines earn ₹45,000 to ₹60,000, while those selected for international airlines can earn upwards of ₹1.5 Lakhs to ₹2 Lakhs per month, often tax-free.
Is swimming compulsory for all Aviation Courses?
Swimming is not compulsory for ground staff or management courses (BBA/MBA). However, it is a mandatory survival skill taught in our Diploma in Cabin Crew Management, as international airlines require flight attendants to know how to swim for emergency water landings (ditching).
